Private-Sector Bank · Core Lending · Cloud Modernization

Case Study I: Containerising Monolithic Credit Infrastructure for a Major Private-Sector Bank

$110K + $90K
Initial ACV + Expansion ARR Contribution

01 - Situation

A prominent private-sector bank was accelerating its digital subprime consumer-lending channel, but its legacy core loan-origination platform ran on a rigid monolithic architecture. Onboarding third-party fintech distribution partners required a three-month technical validation cycle, causing the bank to lose market share to more agile digital-native lenders.

02 - Task

Win the cloud-modernization and containerization contract, compress partner-integration timelines, and protect the core ledger while progressing toward my regional target.

03 - Action

Stakeholder navigation: The deal was gridlocked by the Group CISO and Head of Core Infrastructure, who saw a microservices overhaul as operational risk and potential core-database downtime. I made the strategic decision to leverage an international banking hardware/software enterprise operating as our ISV partner. That partner already held a pre-cleared master service framework inside the bank’s network.

Action: I orchestrated a co-selling motion with the ISV account team and positioned our pre-packaged Cloud-Native Core Modernization product suite as a modular software extension embedded inside the ISV’s pre-cleared middleware. I ran parallel alignment tracks: I pitched the Chief Product Officer on how removing partner-onboarding friction would increase acquisition velocity, while demonstrating to the CISO that the containerized setup isolated the core ledger from external system issues.

04 - Result

We accelerated through the three-month InfoSec validation gate using the pre-cleared partner framework and moved the bank to real-time activation. The deployment delivered an additional $90K ARR expansion contribution, reduced partner-integration time by 4×, and lowered infrastructure cost structures by 35%.

Subprime Digital Lending · Unified Data Platform

Case Study II: Deploying a Unified Data Platform for a Subprime Digital Lender

$90K ARR
Product Agreement Executed

01 - Situation

A high-volume digital subprime lender had severe operational blindness. Years of transaction logs, risk models, and account profiles were trapped across 27 disconnected legacy databases. Underwriting engines could not assess customer credit risk in real time, driving elevated default rates and high infrastructure-maintenance overhead.

02 - Task

Position and sell our standardized Unified Data Architecture Platform product to consolidate the databases, unlock real-time underwriting intelligence, and secure an enterprise software win.

03 - Action

Stakeholder navigation: The commercial hurdle was a budget clash. The Head of Data refused to fund a speculative, long-cycle data-engineering project, while the Head of Risk Analytics urgently needed real-time data to stop credit leakage. I made the strategic decision to run a rigorous cost-of-inaction audit and shift the narrative from component pricing to immediate balance-sheet protection.

Action: My solution-architect team analyzed historical default logs and built a quantitative model proving that data silos were directly responsible for annual collection leaks. I then presented our pre-packaged Fintech Data Platform product running on a unified Snowflake and Databricks architecture. I brought the VP of Business Intelligence into an active sandbox and demonstrated how the product could ingest historical records to provide a single, compliant 360-degree customer view out of the box, without a custom ground-up build.

04 - Result

We won the Head of Data’s sign-off by proving the platform would deliver an immediate 60% reduction in raw infrastructure overhead costs. The $90K ARR product agreement was executed, onboarding over 2.7M assets and improving repayment efficiency by 23%.

Case Study III: Landing a Tier-1 Anchor Financial Institution via SaaS Data-Ingestion APIs

$400K ARR
Flagship Enterprise Contract

01 - Situation

While managing the enterprise account portfolio for a cloud-based B2B ad-intelligence SaaS platform, I identified a strategic opportunity within Mashreq Bank. The bank’s digital-banking and acquisition teams were manually gathering competitor ad-intelligence, media-spending trends, and cross-border audience data. This created a visibility lag that prevented marketing product teams from adjusting performance spend in real time and inflated customer acquisition costs.

02 - Task

Navigate Mashreq’s rigid enterprise procurement framework, secure C-suite buy-in, and close a standardized SaaS Data-Ingestion API platform contract valued at $400K ARR.

03 - Action

Stakeholder navigation: I secured initial validation from Digital Acquisition Leads, but the deal was frozen by the Group Procurement Board and Legal Compliance Committee. They cited a strict moratorium on onboarding new cloud vendors unless the vendor underwent an exhaustive six-month compliance audit. I made the strategic decision to elevate the conversation to the Group Head of Growth & Marketing, while running compliance work in parallel rather than treating procurement as a sequential gate.

Action: I built a comprehensive Business Value Case for the executive steering committee. I shifted the narrative to direct P&L impact, proving that real-time ad-tech ingestion APIs would reduce digital-media waste and CAC by over 30% within 90 days. Once the Group Head recognized the cost of remaining on the manual system, he exercised executive sponsorship. I then worked closely with InfoSec Architects to demonstrate that the platform delivered clean, anonymized competitive data streams via an encrypted RESTful API setup, without touching or accessing the bank’s sensitive customer databases.

04 - Result

We compressed a standard six-month compliance cycle into an expedited approval and executed the $400K ARR enterprise contract at full profit margin. This became the flagship win in my portfolio. The strategic execution and understanding of Mashreq’s commercial ecosystem directly resulted in Mashreq subsequently hiring me to run its outward-facing Developer API Marketplace portfolio.

Regional Commercial Bank · Cross-Border API Clearing

Case Study IV: Engineering a Cross-Border API Clearing Layer for a Regional Financial Institution

$300K ARR
Enterprise Agreement Signed (while at Mashreq)

01 - Situation

While at Mashreq, I led the sale of our B2B API Infrastructure Suite to a prominent regional commercial bank operating across borders. The institution relied on legacy batch-processed clearing loops that introduced a mandatory 24-hour verification lag for high-value cross-border transactions. That latency created capital-allocation friction, limited cash-flow visibility for corporate clients, and exposed the bank to overnight FX volatility risk.

02 - Task

Navigate central-bank compliance frameworks across two national jurisdictions, secure adoption of our standardized API infrastructure suite, and retire portfolio-expansion revenue quotas.

03 - Action

Stakeholder navigation: The technical verification phase hit gridlock. The client’s Group Head of Risk & Compliance issued a hard veto, arguing that moving cross-border ledger calls to an automated real-time external API gateway exposed the bank to multi-party fraud liability and violated regional data-isolation mandates. I held parallel validation tracks: our internal regulatory-compliance officers isolated policy concerns while I aligned the client’s technology and business decision-makers.

Action: I organized a dedicated technical sandbox simulation for the CIO and Lead Platform Architects. I demonstrated our Dual API Suite, real-time Payment Initiation and secure Statement Verification APIs, operating as a unified system-to-system clearing mechanism. The product executed an automated encrypted verification handshake to validate systemic compliance before any automated payout payload could trigger, removing manual-tampering risk. To satisfy the risk team, I worked with engineering heads to design a localized, read-only secure transaction-log route, achieving compliance without touching the bank’s core domestic ledger database.

04 - Result

We broke the multi-jurisdictional compliance gridlock and aligned all internal stakeholders. The automated API framework went live across the corporate settlement portfolio, compressing trade-clearing loops from 24 hours to a secure sub-second execution window with 100% real-time tracking visibility. The enterprise agreement was signed at $300K ARR, serving as a benchmark win toward my overall expansion goals.
